"My goal is to make myself unnecessary."

(Information about my background is available on the About Me page.)
My goal for every student is to help improve their problem solving skills. My core principles are:

  • Exercises v. Problems: Oftentimes, we relegate the idea of math to mere memerization of procedure. That is not to say these "exercises" of procedure aren't important; sometimes, it is good to know how to do something (like solve elementary derivatives) fast. However, math instruction should always culminate in being able to solve "problems", questions which are unfamiliar and require you to apply your knowledge in a new way. I'll try to make sure that we always conclude topical lessons by attacking genuine problems.
  • Evaluation is key: Regular assessment helps track progress and identify areas for improvement. I'll provide problem-sets and review material so that you can assure yourself that you are making progress.
  • Communication and Feedback: This is the other side of evalution. I will provide feedback on your work, and I expect you to do the same. Each session is a two-way street, and to make sure you're getting the most out of each session, you should feel free to ask for clarification until you truly grasp the subject.
As a tutor, I want my students to be better problem solvers. Mathematics can be difficult, but if you apply yourself, refrain from taking mental shortcuts, and are willing to work hard, you can improve your math skills and become more independent in your learning (And, as a bonus, improve your scores and grades.). One day, I hope each of my students will be able to walk into a difficult class and feel confident in their ability to understand new and challenging concepts on their own.





What I Offer

Subject Areas

I am a math tutor. I can help you in the following subject areas:

  • High School Math: Geometry, Algebra, Calculus, Statistics (Baisically, anything up to the equivalent of Calculus 2/Intro to Stats)
  • Standardized Tests: AP Calc AB/BC, AP Precalculus, SAT/ACT
  • Math Competitions: Contests of rigor up to and including AMC 10 (ie. MathCOUNTS, AMC 8, AMC 10)

Type of Tutoring

I offer in-person tutoring only in the summer for students who live nearby. If you're a resident of the Inland Empire/San Bernadino, Calif., I may be able to help you!
I offer online tutoring year-round. Lessons are individual only. (I am going to try rolling out group lessons for SAT/ACT prep in the future. If you want more info, contact me and I'll keep you updated.)

Materials

For some subjects, (SAT prep, AP Calculus), I have material that we can use to prepare directly.
For other subjects, I rely on texts and other resources from 3rd parties. For transparency and your reference, I've listed them below:

  • Introduction to Algebra, 2nd Ed., by Richard Rusczyk (2015)
  • Precalculus, 2nd Ed., by Richard Rusczyk (2009)
  • Calculus, Early Transcendentals 8th Ed., by James Stewart (2016)
  • The Art and Craft of Problem Solving, by Paul Zeitz (2007)
